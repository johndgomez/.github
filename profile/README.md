**Enterprise software design and evolution**

The professional practice of [John D. Gómez](https://github.com/johndgomezh), focused on software design, enterprise integration and the evolution of systems that support business operations.

## Where the work begins

**A consequential design decision.** Examine responsibilities, dependencies and implementation options before committing to a direction.

**An integration across business systems.** Clarify information contracts, ownership and recovery when only part of a workflow succeeds.

**An existing system that needs to evolve.** Plan incremental changes around testing, delivery, maintainability and operational continuity.
