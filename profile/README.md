**Enterprise software design and evolution**

Software design, integration and modernization for systems that support business operations. This organization brings together the professional work of [John D. Gómez](https://github.com/johndgomezh).

## Areas of work

**Technical design.** Define responsibilities, examine dependencies and compare implementation options before committing to a change.

**Enterprise integration.** Connect applications and business workflows through clear information contracts, with explicit handling of partial failures.

**System evolution.** Plan incremental changes to existing software, considering testing, delivery, maintainability and operational continuity.
